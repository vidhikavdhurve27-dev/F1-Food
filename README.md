# F1+Food

## Predict • Rescue • Feed

F1+Food is an AI-powered verified food rescue platform that reduces food waste by intelligently connecting verified food providers with NGOs before food is wasted.

---

# Problem Statement

Large amounts of edible food are wasted every day from:

- Restaurants
- Hotels
- Temples
- College Messes
- Caterers
- Corporate Cafeterias

Meanwhile, NGOs and shelters struggle to obtain food in time.

---

# Our Solution

F1+Food uses Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to predict surplus food, identify food type, estimate quantity, recommend suitable NGOs, optimize pickup routes, and provide live tracking of donation vehicles.
